Program

Half-day workshop built around open problems

The program combines four invited talks, workshop-paper spotlights, posters, and a moderated panel, with ample time for audience discussion.

November 12, 2026 · 2:00–6:00 PM Interactive panel discussion

Invited Speakers

Alan Fern

Oregon State University

Planning and learning under uncertainty, reinforcement learning, and decision making for robots.

Tao Pang

Robotics and AI Institute

Contact-rich manipulation, model-based reasoning, and robust physical interaction.

Pulkit Agrawal

MIT

Robot learning, legged mobility, manipulation, generalization, and sim-to-real transfer.

Emo Todorov

University of Washington and Roboti

Optimal control, physics simulation, contact dynamics, and robot movement.
